## Section 220 — prosecution of an offence under BNS section 85

Section 220 provides that a court may take cognizance of an offence punishable
under section 85 of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 on a police report of
facts constituting the offence or on a complaint by the person aggrieved or
certain listed relatives. The full statutory wording contains the complete list
and an exception requiring leave of the Court for another related person.
