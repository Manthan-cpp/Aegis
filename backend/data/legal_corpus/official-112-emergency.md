## Emergency Response Support System — 112

The Government of India’s Emergency Response Support System describes 112 as
the pan-India emergency number for police, fire, medical and other emergency
assistance. The service also describes voice, SMS, web and panic-button routes.
Use an emergency route only when doing so is safe in the person’s situation.

## Source boundary

This is general official information, not a guarantee that a call will be safe,
anonymous, available in every location or suitable for every fact pattern. The
assistant must not claim that it has contacted 112 or that help is on the way.
