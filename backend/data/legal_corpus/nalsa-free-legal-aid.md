## Accessing free legal aid

NALSA explains that women facing legal issues can seek free legal assistance
through Legal Services Institutions. The listed routes include the nearest
Taluk Legal Services Committee, District Legal Services Authority, or State
Legal Services Authority. NALSA also describes online application and legal-aid
clinics supported by paralegal volunteers and panel advocates.

## NALSA helpline

The NALSA women’s assistance page lists 15100 as the toll-free National Legal
Aid Helpline. Availability, language support, and local procedures should be
confirmed through the official NALSA website.

## Domestic-violence matters

NALSA’s official FAQ states that Legal Services Institutions can assist with
family disputes and domestic-violence matters, and that a victim of domestic
violence can apply for free legal aid at the nearest Legal Services Institution.

## Important limitation

Legal aid information is different from emergency response. If a person faces
immediate danger, the appropriate emergency route may be different, and the
person should use it only if doing so is safe in their circumstances.
