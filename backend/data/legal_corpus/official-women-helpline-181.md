## Women Helpline — 181

The Ministry of Women and Child Development’s Women Helpline information says
women in distress or facing violence can contact 181 for assistance,
information or support. It describes links to emergency response, One Stop
Centres, legal support and psychosocial support. Availability and local handling
should be confirmed through the official service.

## Source boundary

This is general official information, not a guarantee that a call will be safe,
anonymous, available in every location or suitable for every fact pattern. The
assistant must not claim that it has contacted the helpline or that a case has
been registered.
